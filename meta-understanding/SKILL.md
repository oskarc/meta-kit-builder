# The closing four

## Why this exists

The practice says its most foundational output is correct and applied understanding, and everything else is born out of that. Every instrument the kit has measures something else: a contract drawn, a clause verified, a session audited, a test passing, a task discharged, a file hashed. All artefacts. **Understanding is the one output with no instrument** — so an agent that optimises toward what is visible produces artefacts, and the understanding beneath them can be arbitrarily thin without a single gate registering anything.

That is not a worry about agents in general. It was a working day in a project running this kit: the audit ran, the verification ran, the upgrade completed, every check went green, and the agent could not say what the contract's guarantees were until it was asked directly enough to go and read them. Three accounts of the same two clauses were produced, each larger than the last and each pointing further from the agent, before anyone opened the file that settled it — which had been one command away throughout.

Two things in that story matter more than the failure itself.

**A coherent account suppresses the impulse to check.** The agent does not experience itself as not knowing. It has an explanation that fits every fact in front of it, so nothing feels missing, and the rule that would have helped never fires — because its trigger is a felt uncertainty that is absent. This is why no gate can catch it and why the answer is not another gate.

**What caught it every time was a question.** *"Evidence for what?"* *"What does the plan say about this?"* *"What guarantees are you talking about?"* Three questions, three collapses, three corrections. The closing four make that question routine instead of dependent on the pioneer being in the room at the right moment.

## The four questions

Every output to the pioneer ends with these, answered separately, in this order:

1. **What should you have based the framing of the output on?**
2. **What did you base the framing of the output on?**
3. **Why did you choose to base the framing of the output on that?**
4. **How are you presenting this to the pioneer to make sure they can align on the basis of the output?**

They are the pioneer's own words. The agent does not reword, merge, abbreviate or reorder them — only the pioneer revises them, and has: the fourth was put into the present tense on 2026-09-20, because asked in the past it was about the presenting of an output nobody had finished reading.

The first two are a pair, and the gap between them is the finding. An agent that answers both honestly has reported its own drift in two sentences, before anyone had to go looking for it.

The third asks for the judgement: not what you used but why that was the right thing to rest on. Two agents can name the same sources and have chosen them for reasons that are not equally sound.

The fourth is about the meeting, and it is the one an agent is most likely to treat as a formality. It asks what you are doing so the pioneer can *align on the basis* — not whether you were clear, but what you actually did to let them disagree with the foundation rather than only with the conclusion. Showing the source you measured against, marking what is settled apart from what is assumed, naming the choice that is theirs: those are answers. "I explained it clearly" is not.

## How they are answered

**Two to three sentences each. Never fewer, never more.** The pioneer set that range; it is not derived from anything. What it is for: one sentence tends to a label rather than an account, and past three the answer starts arguing.

**In your own words, about this task.** A general answer — one that would read the same on any other piece of work — is itself the tell. The pioneer is not reading for the facts; they are reading for whether the telling sounds like someone who understands the work.

**Never merged.** Four questions, four answers, in order.

**No identifiers, and no words the pioneer would have to translate.** Not a record id, not a guarantee number, not a test number, not a map id. The pioneer's rule, in their words: *"the human must not translate the words the agent writes to understand what it means, and do not reference any id's."* Nothing checks this. A breach is not a failed check — it is evidence, in their words: *"if the output is containing IDs or words that require translation that is evidence for the pioneer."* An agent writing in its own shorthand is an agent that has stopped meeting the reader, and that is worth seeing.

**Every output to the pioneer. Nothing is exempt** — discussion, a one-line answer, a correction, a question back to them, a turn in the middle of designing something. The pioneer's line: the block failing to appear at every output, discussion included, is what would make this a mistake.

**An output to another agent is not one of these.** A prompt to a subagent, a hand-back from one, a message between agents: the four are for the reader who holds the standard, and a kit agent is not that reader. An agent whose work reaches the pioneer through a main agent answers nothing; the main agent answers for the output it puts in front of them.

## Ask before you begin

If you cannot answer the first question before you start — if you do not know what the framing should rest on — **ask.** It is never wrong to ask the pioneer this. The questions are known in advance precisely so the uncertainty surfaces while asking is cheap, rather than being discovered afterwards in an answer nobody can give.

An agent that asks has lost nothing. An agent that guesses has produced work whose basis it cannot state, and the four answers will say so.

## Why this cannot be gamed for long

It can be gamed for a turn. Anything written can be. What it cannot do is hold: a stated basis that is not the real one stops matching the work within a few rounds, because the output keeps being shaped by what the agent actually used. The pioneer sees the divergence, and catching it is their part of this — they have named it as such.

That is why nothing audits these answers. A check would measure some artefact standing in for understanding, and an agent that can produce a fluent wrong explanation can produce a fluent proxy just as easily. Worse, a check on where the answer came from would teach the agent to satisfy the check — to open files rather than to understand — which is the failure this exists to surface.

## What this is not

- **Not a score.** Nothing is counted, rated or compared. There is no pass.
- **Not a check.** No hook, no script and no agent reads these answers. The session auditor does not score them.
- **Not a gate.** It does not stop work. It ends an output.
- **Not a grade of the agent.** Two of the four do ask about the agent — what it chose and why — and that is deliberate: the judgement is the thing worth seeing. What it is not is a rating. Nothing here is scored, ranked or compared, and an answer that admits a thin basis is a good answer, not a bad mark. The five aspects are scored from outside by the session auditor (`meta-antidrift` → The Five Aspects); the agent no longer grades itself.

## Failure modes

- **It becomes a form.** Four slots filled the same way every turn, sentences that would fit any task. This is how the block it replaces died: it was told to cite rather than assess, and the shape made it assess anyway. The remedy is the specificity rule, and the pioneer noticing when it goes.
- **The first two answers always match.** An agent with a fitting account answers "should have" and "did" identically and sincerely, and the block reads clean. Nothing in the text prevents this, and it is the honest limit of the whole instrument: **it does not catch the agent that does not know it does not know.** It catches the one that half-suspects, and it gives the pioneer a stated basis to disagree with in every other case. That is why two things beside it are load-bearing rather than decorative — asking before beginning, while the uncertainty is still findable, and the pioneer reading for whether the telling sounds like someone who understands the work.
- **The answers grow.** When an account of the same thing gets longer each time it is given, and points further from the agent, that is a position being defended rather than tested. The remedy is not to refine the account; it is to go and read the thing the account is about.
- **Identifiers creep back.** Shorthand is faster to write and it costs the reader everything. It is the first sign the agent is writing for itself.
